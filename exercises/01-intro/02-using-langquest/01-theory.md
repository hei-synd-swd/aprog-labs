# Working with lq

Not every exercise is code. This one is a **text exercise**: instead of fixing a program, you write an answer in plain Markdown, and LangQuest checks that your answer mentions the key concepts.

A text exercise works just like a code one:

- The file you edit is `main.md` instead of `main.rs`.
- Press `e` to open it in your editor, write your answer **below** the `<!-- Write your answer below -->` line, and save.
- On save, LangQuest re-checks your answer and updates the **Output** page. You pass once you have covered enough of the expected concepts.

This exercise is a short recap of how LangQuest itself works - the same keys and ideas you met in the previous exercise. If you are unsure, remember you can press `h` for a hint (each hint you reveal is recorded), and the shortcut table lives on the Theory page of the first exercise.

Move to the **Task** page (`→`) to see the questions.
